# Product: Reanimator

**One line:** You are a necromancer who raises dead repos; Death comes to break what you revived, and you heal it.

- **Who it's for:** developers (and judges) who like the idea of bringing abandoned projects back to life — and seeing them hardened against a real adversary.
- **The two halves (Frankenstein):** *Reanimate* a dead repo (prove it runs with real vitals), then *Death attacks it* — the Poltergeist engine red-teams the revived app — and you *heal* the findings with Kiro.
- **Kiro is the familiar:** it searches the graveyard, performs the surgery and heals wounds on your command. This is the Kiro-integration story.
- **The look:** a necromancer's dot-matrix vision — everything drawn as dithered dots/glyphs on black, only magic glows (`docs/THEME.md`).
- **Integrity:** nothing is faked for the camera — a pulse means real vitals, a wound means a real finding, a heal means a real fix (`docs/DECISIONS.md` D-R11).

The spec is in `.kiro/specs/reanimator/`. Scope and priorities are in `docs/PRD.md`. The story is in `docs/STORY.md`.
